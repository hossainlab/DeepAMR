Public databases:

CARD (Comprehensive Antibiotic Resistance Database): https://card.mcmaster.ca/
NCBI Pathogen Detection: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pathogens/
ResFinder database: https://cge.food.dtu.dk/services/ResFinder/
PATRIC: https://www.patricbrc.org/

What to download:

Bacterial whole genome sequences (FASTA files)
Corresponding antibiotic susceptibility testing (AST) results
Focus on: E. coli, Klebsiella, Staph aureus, Acinetobacter (common in Bangladesh)
